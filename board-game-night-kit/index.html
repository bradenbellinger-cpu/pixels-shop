How to Play

  1. Shuffle the 24 cards.
  2. Each player takes 6 cards. Rest of deck is stock.
  3. On your turn: play one card (score points), discard one, draw back up to 6.
  4. First to 15 points wins the round. Best of 5 wins the night!

Card Types

  • Score (green): points when played.
  • Steal (red): take an opponent's highest card.
  • Block (blue): stop the next Steal.
  • Wild (yellow): counts as the best card in hand.
