# Pixel's Printable Board Game Night Kit
## Step-by-Step Guide for Printing, Cutting, and Assembling the Kit

Welcome to Pixel's Printable Board Game Night Kit! Follow these steps to print, cut, and assemble your own board game at home using only free tools and materials you already have.

---

### What You Will Need

**Free tools:**
- A printer (inkjet or laser)
- Paper (standard 8.5" x 11" / A4 printer paper, or cardstock if you have it)
- Scissors or a craft knife (box cutter / X-Acto)
- A ruler or straight edge
- A pencil (light, for marking fold lines)
- Glue stick, tape, or clear adhesive
- Optional: a bone folder or the edge of a credit card for crisp folds

**Optional extras (still free if you already own them):**
- A paper trimmer
- A hole punch
- String, ribbon, or binder rings (for cards or booklets)
- A laminator or clear contact paper (to make pieces last longer)

---

### Step 1: Download the Kit Files

1. Open the kit download page you received from Pixel.
2. Download each file (PDF or image) to your computer.
3. Save them in one folder so they are easy to find.
4. Print a test page of the first file on plain paper to check sizing before printing everything.

---

### Step 2: Print the Kit

1. Open the file you want to print.
2. Choose **File → Print**.
3. Set the paper size to match your paper (Letter 8.5" x 11" or A4).
4. Set **Page Scaling** to **Actual Size** or **100%** (do NOT choose "Fit to Page" — this changes the game piece sizes).
5. Choose portrait or landscape orientation as shown in the file preview.
6. Print one page at a time if you are using cardstock or thicker paper.
7. Let the ink dry completely before handling (especially for inkjet printers).

**Tip:** If you have cardstock, print the game board and cards on cardstock for sturdier pieces. Otherwise, print on regular paper and glue it to cardboard from a cereal box.

---

### Step 3: Cut Out the Pieces

1. Lay your printed pages on a flat, safe surface (use a cutting mat or a piece of cardboard underneath to protect your table).
2. Using scissors or a craft knife, cut along the **outer black lines** of each piece.
3. Cut carefully around game cards, tokens, the board, dice, and any characters or accessories.
4. If a piece has a **dotted line**, do NOT cut all the way through — that is a fold line (see Step 4).
5. Cut out any center windows or slots carefully (ask a grown-up for help with craft knives).
6. Trim the corners of cards slightly rounded if you want them to feel like real playing cards.

---

### Step 4: Score and Fold

1. For any piece with a dotted fold line, place your ruler along the line.
2. Lightly run the back of your pencil, a bone folder, or a credit card edge along the line to make a shallow crease (this is called "scoring"). Do not cut through the paper.
3. Fold along the scored line in the direction shown (usually "fold inward" so the printed side stays outside).
4. Press the fold flat with your fingernail or the edge of a ruler for a crisp edge.
5. Repeat for all fold lines on boxes, cards, tokens, and 3D pieces.

---

### Step 5: Assemble the Pieces

**Flat cards and tokens:**
- No assembly needed beyond cutting and folding. If you glued paper to cardboard, trim the cardboard to match the paper shape.

**Foldable boxes and 3D pieces:**
1. Apply glue stick or tape to the tabs marked "glue here."
2. Press the tab firmly against the inside edge of the adjacent side.
3. Hold for a few seconds until the glue grips.
4. Let dry completely before using.

**Game board:**
- If your board prints on multiple pages, lay the pages side by side in the correct order.
- Tape the backs together with clear tape, or glue them to a larger piece of cardboard.
- Trim any uneven edges.

**Dice:**
1. Cut out the cube shape.
2. Score all fold lines.
3. Fold into a cube and glue the tabs.
4. Let dry before rolling.

---

### Step 6: Organize Your Game Night Kit

1. Sort pieces into piles: board, cards, tokens, dice, rules, and players.
2. Store cards in a small box, envelope, or zip-top bag.
3. Keep the rule sheet handy so everyone can learn how to play.
4. If you made a box for the kit, label it "Pixel's Board Game Night Kit" on the outside.

---

### Step 7: Play!

1. Gather your friends or family.
2. Read the rules out loud together.
3. Set up the board, shuffle the cards, and roll the dice.
4. Have fun — and don't forget to cheer when someone wins!

---

### Safety Notes

- Scissors and craft knives are sharp. Always cut away from your body and ask a grown-up for help if you are young or unsure.
- Glue and tape can be messy — cover your work surface with scrap paper.
- Do not put small game pieces in your mouth; they are a choking hazard for young children and pets.
