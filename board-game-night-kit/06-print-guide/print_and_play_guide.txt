Pixel's Printable Board Game Night Kit — Print & Play Guide

PAPER CHOICES
• Board & Box: 200–300 gsm matte cardstock (≈ 110–130 lb cover) — sturdy, folds cleanly, resists wear.
• Cards: 250–300 gsm linen-finish cardstock — good shuffle feel, hides fingerprints.
• Tokens & Dials: 200 gsm cardstock + optional self-adhesive label paper for stick-on tokens.
• Dice Templates: 160–200 gsm cardstock — folds without cracking, holds shape.
• Score Pads: 80–100 gsm offset/printer paper — tears cleanly, writes well.
• Home Printer Friendly: All files include 3 mm bleed; print on A4 or US Letter at 100 % (do not scale).

CUTTING TIPS
• Tools: Steel ruler, self-healing mat, fresh craft knife (X-Acto #11) or rotary cutter. Scissors only for curves.
• Order: Cut outer edges first, then internal slots/holes. Score fold lines before cutting dice tabs.
• Scoring: Use the back of the knife blade or a bone folder; fold toward the printed side for valley folds, away for mountain folds.
• Dice: Cut outline, score every tab line, fold tabs inward, glue tabs with PVA or glue stick — hold 10 s each.
• Cards: Cut full sheet first, then trim individual cards for square corners. Round corners with corner punch if desired.
• Tokens: Punch 19 mm circles with archival punch, or cut squares and round by hand.

STORAGE & ORGANIZATION
• Deck Boxes: Repurpose TCG deck boxes (100-card size) for each card type; label with included printable labels.
• Token Trays: Use bead organizers, pill boxes, or print the fold-up token tray template (included in Token Sheets).
• Dice Bag: Small drawstring pouch or print the fold-up dice tray.
• Score Pad: Keep tear-off pad in a clipboard or folder with the rule booklet.
• Master Box: A standard board game box (30 × 30 × 5 cm) holds boards, boxes, trays, and pads flat.
• Travel: Everything fits in a zip-lock bag + deck boxes for cafe play.
